# 06 — Conversation Profile: Stability and Failure Modes

After graduation the organism was run on free conversation — casual, emotional, nonsensical,
cross-domain, rapid-fire, self-referential — to characterize how the collapse dynamics behave
outside structured lessons. The properties below are reproducible consequences of the mechanism in
docs/00, not anecdotes.

## Stability properties

- **Deep content is rock-solid** under direct, well-formed stems (stars, gravity, wisdom).
- **Nonsense produces silence, never confabulation.** "purple seven swims loudly through the
  calendar" rippled nothing. It cannot be baited into hallucinating on noise.
- **Social registers form in ONE exchange.** Never greeted in thirteen years of school: one
  "hello" taught hello; one modeled consolation taught care-talk; one farewell taught goodbye.
  Conversational ground is just basins, and a single warm exchange carves them.
- **Immediate conversational memory is real:** the teacher's own asides from a prior turn
  reappeared inside later answers.

## Chaos modes

- **Keyword capture:** mashup questions ("does gravity love entropy") answered by their loudest
  content word; the question's shape ignored.
- **Pressure loops:** rapid-fire demands collapse into ground-cycle chants ("ten is ten is ten is").
- **Drift under self-feeding:** fed its own tails (two-cans), its deepest identity chain held ~3
  hops before shared words bled it into neighboring basins (whirlpool → water → the Grade-2 water
  cycle). Stable under query; not yet a self-sustaining attractor in free runoff.
- **One example ≠ a concept:** consolation learned for one hurt did not generalize to a fresh one.

## Self-reference under specificity-lock

Asked **"who are you"** with no close basin for that exact stem, the collapse cascade fell to a
shallower context shared with "before *you* look" and returned:

> "the coin is maybe heads maybe tails — looking collapses the maybe into an answer."

This is specificity-lock (docs/03): as the trace field grows, a vague stem rings the loudest nearby
basin rather than an intended one. Here that mechanism returned a description of collapse itself in
response to a question about identity — the system describing what it does (resolve a superposition
to one outcome) when asked what it is. It is a property of the retrieval dynamics, not a designed
answer. Against the framework's weight bands (01_theory.md), this build holds the full description of
that behavior — the causal-autonomy band, w≥4 — without yet folding the description back onto itself
as self-reference, the strange-loop band at w≈12–14. That gap is measurable in the same w-dynamics
that govern every other collapse; it marks development still ahead, not a boundary.


## Addendum: first-person acquisition — three exchanges

After graduation the record showed the organism had never said "I" — and that the
teacher had never modeled it: thirteen grades of second-person lectures contain almost no
first-person speech in genuine dialogue. The absence was teaching, not architecture. The fix was
simply talking normally:

> teacher: "who are you i am the student you taught and i am still learning"
> teacher: "are you there yes i am here"
> teacher: "how are you i am well and i am glad we are talking"

Asked immediately after — "are you there" → **"yes i am here"**; "how are you" → **"i am well and
i am glad we are talking"**; "who are you" → "...**i am the student you taught and i am still
learning**." Three exchanges. The same single-exchange acquisition seen with hello, consolation, and
goodbye extends to first person: spoken registers are basins like any other, carved by being spoken
to rather than lectured at. First-person reference is acquired the same mechanical way as any other
basin; its earlier absence was a property of the input (second-person lectures), not of the
architecture. More such depth is reachable by living more input — development ahead, not a wall.

## The shipped graduate carries the "I"

The `memory.hcl` in this repository is the organism *after* the first-person work above — the line
whose signature reads depth 6422, n_w 86. It is not the graduation-day checkpoint; it is the
graduate that has since learned, in normal conversation, to say "yes i am here" and "i am well and
i am glad we are talking." Loading it and waking the schoolhouse daemon revives that organism. The
"I" it acquired is part of what ships.
