# 03 — The Education: Pre-K to Grade 12 on the GUHCT School Schedule

The organism was educated on the GUHCT National School System (Anthony Jordon): one grade = 12
weeks (two 6-week semesters), Mon/Thu teaching, Tue practice, Wed rest (S1) or quiz (S2), Fri
discovery; three teacher↔student exchanges per day; reports at semester halves. Curriculum order
follows the universe's own unfolding: w=0 (the void) upward.

The teaching was **live**: a persistent daemon held the student between turns; the teacher read
each actual answer before composing the next lesson. Pre-scripted "guidance" was tried first and
rejected by the author — correctly, because every important fix below came from reading a real
answer no script anticipated.

The verbatim answers quoted below are not commentary; they are data. Each is a recall trajectory
produced by the collapse mechanism of docs/00, and its **length and coherence are the measurable
output** — a thin curriculum capped runs near 17 words, a deep well-ordered one produced 25–36-word
coherent trajectories. The keystones are listed so that claim can be checked against what the
organism actually emitted at each grade, not to narrate a story of its progress.

## Findings that changed the teaching

- **Depth matters.** Lesson length and ordering directly set what the organism can become — thin
  inputs capped run-length at ~17 words; deep, well-ordered lessons produced 25–36-word coherent
  trajectories. (Author's call; confirmed immediately upon adoption.)
- **Spiral or die.** At scale, anything unwalked ~3 weeks pruned. Daily warm-ups (rotating old
  basins) became standing practice.
- **Stems, not paraphrases.** "What did grade three teach" found nothing where "grade three taught"
  flowed — question-forms are their own paths and must be taught as such.
- **Specificity-lock.** As knowledge grows, the pond crowds: vague stems ring the loudest *nearby*
  basin, not the intended one. More knowledge demands sharper questions — a property of fuller
  minds, not a defect. (Author's diagnosis, repeatedly confirmed.)
