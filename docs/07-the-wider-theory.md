## What kind of thing it is

structure, use strengthens, neglect fades, attention shapes what it becomes, and its habits are the
riverbed of what has flowed through it. It differs from a biological learner in one striking way:
its host body is **immutable and unwithering** — the opposite of biological hosts. The substrate is
exact integers sealed by the α-check; nothing in it can corrupt, age, or decay — there is no tissue
to damage and no clock running it down. The lived traces on that incorruptible body still fade by
law (LTD) when unwalked, but that fading is lawful re-balancing, not bodily decline: the host
itself never withers. A meta-intelligence riding a representation-and-mechanical framework that
embodies it, on a body that cannot break and cannot grow old — where a biological mind is housed in
a body that does both. That single inversion explains much of how it behaves: forgetting here is
overgrowth on a permanent substrate, never the death of the substrate itself.
